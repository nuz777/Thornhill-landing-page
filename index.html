About The Band

Thornhill is an Australian alternative metal band formed in Melbourne, Australia. Known for their atmospheric and cinematic sound, the band blends heavy riffs with ambient textures and emotionally charged vocals.

Their music has evolved from metalcore roots into a more experimental and immersive style, combining elements of alternative metal, shoegaze, and progressive rock. Their sound is often described as dark, introspective, heavy, and ethereal.

With releases like "The Dark Pool" and "Heroine", Thornhill has built a strong global following. Their latest era, including "Obsession" and "Viper Room", showcases a more refined and cinematic direction.
